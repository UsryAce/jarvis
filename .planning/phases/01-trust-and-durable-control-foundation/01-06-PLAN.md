<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| local browser/process -> FastAPI | Loopback callers and browser origins are untrusted until server-side session/scope verification. |
| browser cookie -> mutation | Cookie authentication alone does not establish intent; exact Origin and synchronizer CSRF are required. |
| long-lived transport -> session/control truth | Revocation/expiry/stop may change after the handshake. |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-SC | Tampering | package-manager boundary | mitigate | No install; import only locked Phase 1 security/store contracts. |
| T-01-19 | Spoofing | loopback caller/session replay | mitigate | Opaque digest-at-rest sessions, expiry, rotation, revocation and scope checks. |
| T-01-20 | Tampering | malicious local Origin/CSRF | mitigate | Exact Origin, SameSite, synchronizer header, explicit credentialed CORS and no blind retry. |
| T-01-21 | Elevation | unclassified alias/stream/audio/WS | mitigate | Central default-deny real-route inventory and pre-handler/pre-accept checks. |
| T-01-22 | Elevation | revoked long-lived session | mitigate | Bounded session/control rechecks and safe stream/WS termination. |
| T-01-23 | Information Disclosure | auth/access errors | mitigate | Generic stable `SafeError` envelopes and redacted audit events only. |
</threat_model>
