<threat_model>

Trust boundaries: proposal-to-resolver, sealed action-to-policy, issuer-to-public verifier, and verifier-to-ControlStore transaction.

| Threat IDs | STRIDE | Abuse path | Disposition and mitigation | Verification |
|---|---|---|---|---|

| T-03, T-05 | Spoofing/Tampering | stale or replayed approval executes changed action | mitigate: bind every claim and atomically consume/reserve | `tests/test_approval_envelope.py` |
| T-04 | Elevation | verifier reaches issuer/private material | mitigate: dependency-graph assertions and public-only constructor | `tests/test_approval_envelope.py` |

Residual risk: cryptographic implementation and real multi-process storage behavior remain consumers of these contracts; Plans 02-06, 02-08, and 02-09 must make the import gates strict. High-severity failures block those plans.
</threat_model>
